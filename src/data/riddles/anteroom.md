Your treasure isn't somewhere grand.

No climbing.
No secret passage.
Just a little journey downward
into one of the quieter corners of our home.

It used to be nearly empty—
but time has a funny way
of turning forgotten spaces
into places full of things we need.

So bend low, brave explorer,
and search among the things
that are waiting to be useful.
